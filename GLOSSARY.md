# Pong Redux

A browser Pong game, played on its own or as the Easter egg on waynetellis.com. Site-level terms (Easter egg, Workshop, Zone) belong to the portfolio site's `CONTEXT.md`; this glossary owns only the game's language.

## Game modes

**Game mode**:
One of the four ways to play, chosen on the landing screen: vs CPU, 2 Players, Rugby (1P), Rugby (2P).
_Avoid_: level, game type

**vs CPU**:
One player against the computer-controlled paddle.
_Avoid_: single player, 1P, solo

**2 Players**:
Two people on one device, each controlling one paddle.
_Avoid_: versus, PvP, multiplayer

**Rugby mode**:
The variant where paddle movement adds spin and speed to the ball, long rallies raise a score multiplier, and the game ends at a target score or time limit. Played as Rugby (1P) against the CPU or Rugby (2P) head to head.
_Avoid_: rugby physics, chaos mode

## Where it runs

**Embedded**:
Pong running inside the portfolio's Easter egg page, where the portfolio supplies the way back to the site.
_Avoid_: iframe mode, in-site

**Standalone**:
Pong opened on its own, at its own address or from a phone's home screen, where it supplies its own quiet link back to the site.
_Avoid_: direct mode, app mode

## Playing on a phone

**Touch zone**:
The half of the screen that controls one paddle: the left half controls the left paddle, the right half the right paddle, in every mode.
_Avoid_: control area, touch pad

**Drag control**:
Moving a paddle by dragging a thumb anywhere in its Touch zone; the paddle moves as far as the thumb moves, never jumping to the thumb's position.
_Avoid_: swipe, touch-to-position

**Rotate prompt**:
The screen shown when a phone is held upright, asking the player to turn it to landscape. Showing it during a game pauses the game.
_Avoid_: orientation warning

**Touch layout**:
The menu layout used on touch screens, designed for thumbs first. Same screens and options as the Desktop layout.
_Avoid_: mobile menu, mobile version

**Desktop layout**:
The menu layout used with a mouse and keyboard.
_Avoid_: PC menu
